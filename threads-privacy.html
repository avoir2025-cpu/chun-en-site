Cainergy Privacy Policy

Cainergy 隱私權政策

Cainergy 采能（以下稱「本工具」）由京珀彩妝有限公司（CHUN.EN，以下稱「本公司」）營運，用來搜尋 Threads 上與本公司服務相關的公開貼文，再由同仁親自回覆。本政策說明本工具透過 Threads API 取得哪些資料，以及如何使用、保存與刪除。本公司網站的其他隱私事項，請見〈隱私權政策〉。

Cainergy ("the tool") is operated by Jingpo Makeup Co., Ltd. (CHUN.EN, "we"). We use it to find public Threads posts related to our services, and our staff reply to them in person. This policy explains what data the tool receives through the Threads API, and how we use, keep and delete it. For our website in general, see our Privacy Policy.

一、取得的資料Data We Receive

  • 連結 Threads 帳號時：該帳號的 Threads 用戶編號、帳號名稱，以及 Threads 核發的存取權杖。When a Threads account is connected: its Threads user ID, username, and the access token issued by Threads.
  • 搜尋時：符合關鍵字的公開貼文內容、作者帳號名稱、發文時間與貼文網址。When searching: the text, author username, time and link of public posts that match a keyword.
  • 我們不取得 Threads 帳號密碼，也不讀取私訊或不公開的內容。We never receive your Threads password, and we do not read direct messages or non-public content.

二、用途How We Use It

  • 在本工具裡列出與本公司服務相關的公開貼文，讓同仁判斷是否回覆。To list public posts related to our services, so our staff can decide whether to reply.
  • 回覆一律由同仁在 Threads App 裡親自發出，本工具不會自動發文、回覆、按讚、追蹤或私訊。All replies are written and sent by our staff in the Threads app. The tool never posts, replies, likes, follows or sends messages automatically.
  • 不出售資料，不提供給第三人作行銷用途，也不用於消費者應用程式。We do not sell this data, share it with third parties for marketing, or use it in any consumer app.

三、保存與安全Retention & Security

  • 存取權杖加密保存，只在搜尋時使用。帳號取消連結後立即刪除。Access tokens are stored encrypted and used only to run searches. They are deleted as soon as the account is disconnected.
  • 搜尋取得的公開貼文資料最多保存 90 天，之後自動刪除。Public post data from searches is kept for up to 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • 資料存放在有存取控管的雲端資料庫，只有本公司授權同仁能看。Data is stored in an access-controlled cloud database that only authorized staff can see.

四、取消連結與刪除Disconnecting & Deletion

  • 您可以隨時在本工具裡按「取消連結」，或到 Threads「設定 → 帳號 → 網站權限」移除 Cainergy。移除後，我們會刪除您的權杖與帳號資料。You can disconnect at any time in the tool, or remove it in Threads under Settings → Account → Website permissions. We then delete your token and account data.
  • 如果您是被搜尋到的貼文作者，希望我們刪除與您有關的資料，請寫信到 contact@chunen.tw，附上貼文網址。If you wrote a post that our search found and want related data deleted, email contact@chunen.tw with the post link.
  • 詳細步驟請見〈資料刪除說明〉。See our Data Deletion page for details.

五、您的權利Your Rights

依中華民國個人資料保護法，您得就您的個人資料行使查詢、閱覽、複製、補充、更正、停止蒐集處理利用及刪除之權利。請以 Email 聯繫本公司，我們將於合理期間內處理。

Under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act, you may request access to, copies of, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, or ask us to stop processing it. Email us and we will respond within a reasonable time.
